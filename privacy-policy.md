## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to provide the Services. Here's everything we collect, organized by category:

### 1.1 Account Data
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Encrypted password hash (we never see your actual password)

### 1.2 Profile Data (Optional)

## 4. AI Processing and Anthropic

**This is an important section. Please read carefully.**

CortexMD uses Anthropic's Claude API to power our AI chat feature. When you interact with the AI:

### 4.1 Your Messages Are Sent to Anthropic
When you send a message to our AI chat, that message is transmitted to Anthropic's servers in the United States for processing. Anthropic returns a response that we then display to you.

### 4.2 What Anthropic Receives
- Your text message
- Context we add (such as your current drug scheme, brain region being viewed)
- Our system prompt that frames the AI as an educational tool

### 4.3 What Anthropic Does With It
According to Anthropic's privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy):
- Anthropic may retain inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and safety purposes
- Anthropic does NOT use commercial API customer data (including CortexMD's API usage) to train their models
- Anthropic processes data in the United States

### 4.4 What This Means for You
Any information you share in the AI chat — including details about your health, medications, symptoms, or personal context — may be temporarily stored on Anthropic's servers. While Anthropic has strong security practices, this represents a data flow outside of our direct control.

**Our recommendation**: Do NOT include personally identifying information (full name, exact birth date, home address, phone number, government ID, insurance details) in AI chat messages. The AI does not need this information to provide educational pharmacological context. You can describe your situation abstractly ("a 30-year-old with MDD on sertraline 100mg") rather than identifiably.

### 4.5 No Zero Data Retention
CortexMD does not currently have a Zero Data Retention (ZDR) agreement with Anthropic. This is a specialized enterprise agreement that requires commercial plan status and direct negotiation with Anthropic. We may pursue ZDR in the future as CortexMD scales, but at this time, your AI chat messages are subject to Anthropic's standard retention policy.

### 4.6 Audit Logging
Every AI interaction is logged to our internal `ai_audit_log` database. This log contains:
- Your user ID
- A SHA-256 hash of your input (not the plain text)
- A truncated version of your input (first 500 characters, for debugging)
- The full AI response
- The model used
- Total tokens consumed
- Safety flags (crisis detection, input risk level)

This audit log is used for:
- Safety review (detecting patterns suggesting crisis or abuse)
- Cost tracking
- Debugging AI errors
- Compliance with California AB 489 and similar AI accountability regulations

Audit logs are retained for 90 days and then automatically deleted unless flagged for safety review.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Where Your Data Lives
- **Primary database**: Supabase PostgreSQL cluster, hosted in the United States
- **File storage**: Supabase Storage, United States
- **Application hosting**: Vercel edge network, with primary execution in the United States
- **AI processing**: Anthropic servers, United States

Your data is stored in the United States. If you are a resident of the European Union, Ukraine, or another jurisdiction with data localization requirements, please see Section 9 for details on international data transfers.

### 5.2 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security practices:

**In transit**:
- All data is transmitted over HTTPS with TLS 1.3
- HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) enforced
- No plain-text communication with our servers

**At rest**:
- Supabase encrypts all stored data with AES-256
- Database backups are encrypted
- Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (never stored as plain text)

**Access controls**:
- Row-Level Security (RLS) enabled on all tables containing user data
- Each user can only access their own records (enforced at the database level)
- Admin access is strictly limited, password-protected, and logged
- No third parties (including CortexMD developers) have access to your data without explicit authorization

**Planned enhancements**:
- End-to-end encryption (E2E) for sensitive fields (genetic data, lab results, symptom scores, treatment history) is planned. When implemented, encryption keys will be derived from your password via PBKDF2 and will never leave your device in unencrypted form.

### 5.3 No Security Is Absolute
While we take security seriously, no system can be 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute protection against sophisticated attacks, insider threats, or vulnerabilities in third-party services. If we discover a breach involving your data, we will notify you without undue delay as required by applicable law.
